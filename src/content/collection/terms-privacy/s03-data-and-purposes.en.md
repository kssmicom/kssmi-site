The legal-basis references below describe GDPR grounds where the GDPR applies. For processing in China, we rely on consent unless a specific exception under applicable Chinese law permits processing without it, such as a legal obligation or necessity for a contract to which the individual is a party. GDPR legitimate interests are not a separate general ground under China's Personal Information Protection Law. A company employee is not automatically a party to the company's contract.

We process different data for different purposes and apply the legal basis appropriate to each activity.

| Activity | Data that may be processed | Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product or project inquiry | Name, business email, inquiry details, product, source page, language, IP address, country, user agent, delivery status and inquiry ID | Respond to the request, prepare a quotation, maintain an inquiry record and protect the form from abuse | Consent. Steps requested before entering into a contract; legitimate interests in B2B correspondence, record keeping and service security; and legal obligations where applicable. |
| WhatsApp or email-link opening | Selected channel, server time, page path, link placement, product SKU where relevant, site language and an “intent” status | Operate and troubleshoot contact entry points and understand which contact channel a visitor deliberately selected | Consent. |
| Consent-based visitor journey | Visitor and session identifiers, pages, timestamps, referrer, campaign parameters, country/city, browser/device information, interaction and engagement data | Website analytics, attribution, performance measurement and service improvement | Consent. |
| Advertising and measurement | Consent state, identifiers, page and conversion events and campaign information handled by configured advertising or analytics providers | Measure advertising effectiveness and, where enabled, support advertising features | Consent. |
| Security and abuse prevention | IP address or a hashed representation, request time, user agent, security-verification data and technical errors | Rate limiting, spam prevention, incident investigation and service security | Legitimate interests and, where applicable, compliance with legal obligations. |
| Direct business communications | Email address, phone/account identifier, message content, attachments and later correspondence | Communicate about quotations, samples, manufacturing projects and business relationships | Steps before contract, contract performance and/or legitimate interests depending on the context. |

Website inquiries may include uploaded files, their names, types, sizes and contents. We process them to review the request, prepare a response and deliver correspondence, using the applicable inquiry or business-communication basis described above. Messages and attachments may be held temporarily in a delivery queue; queued acceptance is distinct from email delivery.

When a Kssmi short link is opened, we may record its code, opening time, country, automated-preview classification and any campaign or recipient reference assigned to the link. We use these records to operate the redirect, prevent abuse and assess distribution of business materials, relying on legitimate interests where the GDPR applies and otherwise on the applicable basis described above. A recipient reference can make a record identifiable; this is separate from anonymous page-view totals. A link may be forwarded or opened by a preview service, so an opening does not prove that a particular person read the material. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests using the privacy contact.

### Sources of data

We may obtain data:

- directly from the person completing a form or sending a message;
- automatically from the browser or network connection, subject to the applicable consent and legal requirements;
- from the page and product context associated with a contact action;
- from a company representative, colleague, distributor or other business contact; and
- from service providers used to deliver, secure or measure the website.

Where personal data is obtained from another source, we provide any additional information required by applicable law within the applicable time.

### Data minimisation

Users should provide only the information reasonably needed for their business request. The inquiry form should not be used to submit sensitive personal data, government identifiers, payment-card data or unnecessary information about another person.

The inquiry form asks for separate consent to process and respond to your request:

> I agree that Shenzhen Kssmi Technology Co., Ltd. may use my contact details, message and attachments to review and respond to this inquiry and manage related project correspondence. I can withdraw this consent at privacy@kssmi.com. This does not enable analytics or advertising.
