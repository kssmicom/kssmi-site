### Your data-protection rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights to:

- receive information about the processing of your personal data;
- request access to your personal data;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request deletion of data;
- request restriction of processing;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive and transmit certain data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time for future processing based on consent; and
- receive safeguards relating to qualifying automated decisions, where applicable.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify identity and protect personal data from unauthorised disclosure.

### Exercising your rights

Send a request to **privacy [at] kssmi [dot] com** and describe the right you wish to exercise. Kssmi will respond within the time required by applicable law. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify identity, clarify the request or protect another person's rights.

### Complaints

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority, including the authority in the country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. We encourage you to contact us first so that we can try to address the concern, but doing so does not remove your right to complain to an authority.

### Consent settings

You can use the website's persistent Cookie Settings control to review, change or withdraw analytics and advertising choices. The control and this notice must use consistent category names and must remain available after a visitor accepts or rejects optional processing.

### Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website, processing activities, suppliers or legal obligations change. The version and last-updated date at the top of the page identify the current notice. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request new consent for a material change.
