We may disclose personal data only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, where required by law, or in connection with the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

### Service providers and recipients

We may use the following categories of recipients and service providers where reasonably necessary to operate, protect, communicate through and measure the website:

| Recipient or service category | Role or function | Data that may be involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers | Website and server hosting, storage, backups and system availability | Technical request data, server logs and stored website records |
| Content-delivery and security providers | Content delivery, proxy services, security checks and abuse prevention | IP address, request metadata, security signals and technical logs |
| Analytics and advertising providers | Consent-based measurement, attribution and advertising functions | Consent state, identifiers, page events and campaign information |
| Communication-platform providers | Communication services deliberately selected by the user | Account identifiers, message content and related communication metadata |
| Media-delivery providers | Delivery and performance of website video or other media | IP address, device/browser information and media-request data |
| Approximate-location service providers | Country-level or regional lookup used for inquiry routing or security | IP address and derived approximate location |
| Email, backup, monitoring and maintenance providers | Business communications, resilience, troubleshooting and system operation | Inquiry data, correspondence, technical logs and backup copies where applicable |

We may also share relevant information with professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or parties to a corporate transaction where an appropriate legal basis and safeguards exist.

### International transfers

Kssmi is established in China. Information submitted directly through the website or a communication channel selected by the user may therefore be processed in China. Some service providers may also process information in countries outside the person's country or the European Economic Area.

Where Chapter V of the GDPR applies to a transfer by a controller or processor, the transfer must use a lawful mechanism appropriate to the circumstances, such as an adequacy decision, applicable European Commission standard contractual clauses, or another safeguard or derogation permitted by law. Supplementary technical and organisational measures are applied where appropriate. A person may contact **privacy [at] kssmi [dot] com** to request information about safeguards relevant to their data, subject to lawful confidentiality restrictions.
