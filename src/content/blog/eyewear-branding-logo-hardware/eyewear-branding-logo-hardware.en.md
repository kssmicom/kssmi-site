## The short answer

**Private-label eyewear is not just sticking a logo onto an existing frame — the logo method, decorative metal thickness, copper wire core, hinge system, and PVD plating decide whether your brand identity survives eyewear scale and fits a 300-pair launch. Before cutting tooling, lock vector artwork in the 2D drawing, check micro-detail scale on emblems, separate custom copper-core MOQs (300 pieces per pattern and finish), and plan customer-supplied retail packaging separately.**

For independent brands moving beyond standard catalog frames, small hardware decisions create big differences on the finished frame. Laser marks, hydraulic-stamped 3D bridges, custom copper temple cores, multi-pass mirror plating, and interior limited-edition numbering each carry specific engineering constraints and minimums that are verified on your approved sample.

## Logo application: laser engraving, printing and metal plaques

**Send vector artwork (AI, PDF, EPS, or SVG) with exact dimensions and placement, because vector files drop straight into your 2D technical drawing so logo position and proportion are signed off before tooling or sampling.**

Kssmi applies logos on temples, frame fronts, and lenses where construction permits. Three standard application routes cover almost every brief:

- **Laser engraving (no-fill or 5-color fill):** Handles typography, icons, and interior text cleanly with either a natural no-fill etch or color fill in **gold, silver, white, black, or red**.
- **Abrasion-resistant printed temple branding:** Custom printed temple logos are engineered for surface wear resistance so marks stay crisp through daily handling and cleaning.
- **Custom metal plaques and emblems:** Where flat engraving does not fit the surface texture or luxury tier, inlaid metal plaques, letter emblems, and branded components are developed to match the silhouette.

When two branding layouts remain open at drawing stage — such as clean branding versus numbered markings — Kssmi can prepare both versions side by side inside the 2D technical drawing for direct comparison. Note that if your brand requests a completely new identity system from scratch, work with a dedicated branding designer first and send finalized vector assets for engineering integration.

## 3D metal ornaments, hydraulic stamping and AI-artwork scale limits

**For raised-relief metal bridges, crests, and filigree ornaments, Kssmi typically produces the 3D relief through hydraulic (oil-press) stamping rather than laser engraving — supporting fine pattern detail down to roughly 1 mm, with exact achievability confirmed at engineering review and on the approved physical sample — and dense AI-generated artwork must be adapted before sampling so tiny voids do not fuse solid.**

A common surprise for founders sending AI-generated crests or intricate monograms is the physics of scale: patterns drawn across a large monitor collapse when shrunk to a 10–20 mm eyewear temple emblem. Because dense interior hairlines lose their gaps at eyewear scale, Kssmi gives an honest scale verdict before work starts and proposes a production adaptation — keeping the outer silhouette and signature motifs while opening spacing and strengthening the lines that survive stamping.

Three metal detailing options expand 3D styling:

- **Hydraulic (oil-press) raised relief:** Creates crisp 3D metal bridges, decorative hinges, and raised logos in gold, silver, chrome, or custom finishes after engineering review of the actual pattern image.
- **Cut-out / openwork badges:** Metal initials and logo marks with through-openings are assessed for edge finish and remaining wall strength whenever they sit near load-bearing hinges or rivets.
- **Crystal and CZ detailing:** Crystal or cubic zirconia stones can be set into metal temples or fronts, with stone seating and plating reviewed on drawings and samples prior to mass production.

## Custom copper wire cores for transparent acetate temples

**When using transparent or translucent cellulose acetate, a custom copper needle (wire core) turns internal temple reinforcement into an exclusive signature feature — with buyer-owned tooling at USD 200 and a separate MOQ of 300 pieces (`pcs`) per pattern and per plating finish (distinct from finished frame pairs).**

Instead of picking only catalog wire cores, Kssmi engineers and manufactures custom patterns directly from your visual concept or vector motif:

- **Dedicated mold & exclusive ownership:** A custom copper needle requires a dedicated **USD 200 mold**, owned by you upon payment and reserved strictly for your brand's projects.
- **Separate 300-piece MOQ per pattern and finish:** Custom copper cores carry their own minimum of **300 pieces (`pcs`) per pattern** (starting around **USD 0.35 per piece** depending on design complexity). Applying a second plating finish — choices include **gold, champagne gold, silver, and gunmetal** — counts as a separate core variant and requires its own 300-piece minimum.
- **Transparent acetate calibration:** Core pattern legibility depends on acetate translucency and temple thickness, both confirmed during engineering review of your chosen [acetate sunglasses](/product/acetate-sunglasses/) colorways.

## Hinges, nose pads and 18-color PVD plating finishes

**Hardware reliability depends on matching hinge barrels, plating passes, and nose pads to your frame material — with 18 PVD plated color options, multi-pass mirror plating at roughly USD 0.60 per pair per pass, and a ~3,000-pair-per-model threshold when specifying an international optical hinge brand.**

Standard 300-pair collections run smoothly on Shenzhen-engineered **3-barrel, 5-barrel, and 7-barrel hinges** calibrated for comparable tension and durability across [metal sunglasses](/product/metal-sunglasses/) and [titanium sunglasses](/product/titanium-sunglasses/). When a buyer explicitly specifies an **international optical hinge brand**, supply carries an independent minimum of roughly **3,000 pairs per model** and cannot be split across different styles.

Two plating and comfort details worth locking early in specification:

- **PVD plating & multi-pass mirror finish:** Metal frames and decorative hardware support **18 PVD plated color options** including gold, rose gold, silver/chrome, and gunmetal. For broad mirror-gloss plated surfaces, flawless luster runs as a **multi-pass plating cycle** — typically **2–3 passes** (occasionally more, with final pass count confirmed in the quotation) charged separately around **USD 0.60 per pair per pass** — adding both plating schedule time and a higher MOQ than standard finishes.
- **Matched nose pads & rivets:** Comfort nose pads and hinge rivets are aligned by color and plating batch on your physical sample. ## Interior temple markings, limited-edition serials and pending trademarks

**Interior markings can carry model codes, colorway references, homologation text, and limited-edition serial numbers — and if your trademark is still under registration, bulk frame production can still advance first when your branding uses a compatible surface logo (such as laser engraving or printed branding), applied at the final production stage once schedules and customer requirements are confirmed.**

Practical guidelines keep interior markings clean and manufacturable:

- **Short serial numbering over verbose strings:** For limited drops, concise formats such as **`No. 001`, `No. 002`, `No. 003`** look cleaner on narrow temple walls than lengthy `No. 001 of 300` strings without inflating individual marking work. Markings can sit symmetrically on both temples or on one temple alone.
- **Compliance & certification text:** If your market requires regulatory marks, standard numbers, or lens-category text printed inside the temple, you supply the exact confirmed wording before final drawing sign-off.
- **Late-stage surface logos vs. integrated hardware:** While compatible **surface logo** methods (laser engraving or printed temple marks) can be scheduled near the final production stage with agreed timing while trademark clearance finishes, hardware built into molds or assembly — such as custom copper wire cores, inlaid metal plaques, or sculpted 3D emblems — must still be finalized before frame production starts.

## FAQ

### 1. What logo file format does Kssmi need to start a branded eyewear sample?

Send vector artwork in AI, PDF, EPS, or SVG format along with exact width/height dimensions and target placement (left temple, right temple, front endpiece, or lens). Vector files allow our engineers to drop your mark directly into the 2D drawing for scale and spacing sign-off.

### 2. What is the difference between laser engraving and hydraulic stamping for eyewear logos?

Laser engraving cuts clean flat surface lines and lettering (available plain or color-filled in gold, silver, white, black, or red). Hydraulic (oil-press) stamping is Kssmi's standard route for raised-relief 3D logos, bridges, and decorative plaques, typically holding fine pattern detail down to roughly 1 mm as confirmed at engineering review and on the approved physical sample.

### 3. Can intricate AI-generated crests or filigree patterns be reproduced 1:1 on temples?

Usually not without engineered simplification. Patterns generated for large screens often feature tight internal details that fill solid at a 10–20 mm eyewear emblem size. We give a scale verdict up front and prepare an adapted line-opened drawing for approval before tooling.

### 4. What is the MOQ and mold fee for a custom temple copper wire core?

A fully custom copper wire core requires a dedicated USD 200 mold (owned exclusively by you once paid) and carries its own minimum of 300 pieces (pcs) per pattern (starting from roughly USD 0.35 per piece). Each plating finish (gold, champagne gold, silver, or gunmetal) counts as a separate variant with its own 300-piece minimum.

### 5. What plating finishes are supported on metal eyewear and temple hardware?

We offer 18 standard PVD plated color options including gold, rose gold, silver/chrome, and gunmetal. Large mirror-gloss plated surfaces run through multi-pass plating (typically 2–3 passes billed separately at roughly USD 0.60 per pair per pass, confirmed in the quotation) and require higher MOQs and extra production lead time.

### 6. When should I specify custom Shenzhen hinges vs. an international hinge brand?

Standard 3-barrel, 5-barrel, and 7-barrel Shenzhen hinges match standard 300-pair frame MOQs cleanly. If your specification mandates a specific international optical hinge brand, supply requires roughly 3,000 pairs per model and cannot be shared across styles.

### 7. Can we add individual limited-edition numbers or CE/regulatory markings inside temples?

Yes. Model numbers, colorway codes, regulatory wording supplied by you, and limited-edition serials can be marked inside temples. Concise serials such as No. 001 look sharper on temple walls and are more practical than long "of 300" formats.

### 8. Can production start if my trademark registration is still pending?

For compatible surface logos (such as laser engraving or printed branding), frame production can proceed on schedule and logo application can be arranged at the final stage once timing and customer requirements are agreed in advance. Integrated or molded hardware such as custom copper wire cores, inlaid plaques, or 3D emblems still must be confirmed before production starts.

### 9. Does Kssmi supply custom boxes, cases, or cleaning cloths?

No. We do not manufacture, supply, or resell custom retail packaging. You order your boxes, cases, cleaning cloths, and warranty cards directly from a specialist packaging supplier and ship them to our factory, where we pack finished eyewear into them free of charge.

### 10. Can branding details on catalog models trigger custom tooling fees?

Changing only logo printing, temple laser engraving, or standard colorways keeps an existing catalog shape intact without frame tooling. Adding raised 3D metal plaques, custom copper wire cores, altered temple geometry, or new front shapes introduces dedicated hardware tooling confirmed in the quotation before payment.
